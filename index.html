Privacy Policy

Brightwheel Media Downloader

Last updated: September 28, 2026

1. Overview

Brightwheel Media Downloader is a browser extension that helps users download photos and videos available to them through the Brightwheel website. This policy explains how the extension handles information.

2. Information Accessed

When you use the extension, it accesses content and page information on the Brightwheel website that is necessary to identify and download media you are authorized to view. This may include media URLs and information needed to filter media by date.

3. How Information Is Used

The extension uses this information to provide media scanning and downloading features at your request. Downloaded files are saved using your browser's download functionality.

4. Payments

Optional lifetime Premium access is offered as a one-time purchase. Payments and license verification are handled through ExtensionPay and its payment processor, Stripe. The extension developer does not receive or store your full payment card details. Please review the privacy policies of ExtensionPay and Stripe for information about their data practices.

5. Data Sharing

The developer does not sell users' Brightwheel media or personal information. Payment and license-related information may be processed by ExtensionPay and Stripe to provide payment and licensing services.

6. Data Retention and Security

Downloaded media is saved through your browser's download functionality. Extension-related settings or license information may be stored locally or processed by ExtensionPay as needed to provide the extension's features.

7. Children's Privacy

This extension is intended for adults authorized to access the relevant Brightwheel account and media. Users are responsible for ensuring they have permission to download and retain any media, particularly media depicting children.

8. Changes and Contact

This policy may be updated as the extension changes.
